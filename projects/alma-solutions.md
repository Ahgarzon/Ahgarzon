ALMA explores how conversational assistants can connect customer attention with the operational steps that follow: qualification, appointments, orders and handoff to a team.

## My contribution

This is my **personal project**. I work on conversational workflow design, voice and messaging integrations, and the automation connecting those conversations with business tools.

## Two connected areas

| Area | Workflow |
|---|---|
| Voice and WhatsApp assistants | Handle inquiries, gather relevant information, support qualification and scheduling, and preserve conversation context |
| Restaurant operations | Understand text or voice orders, consult menu information, calculate order details and prepare the handoff to the kitchen |

The restaurant work also explores vision/OCR to support receipt review. That step assists a verification workflow; it does not establish that a payment settled or eliminate fraud.

## Technology and design

Retell AI, WhatsApp integrations, n8n, NocoDB, LLMs, vision/OCR and reporting tools appear across this work. The focus is keeping conversation, structured data and operational actions connected, with human escalation for exceptions.

## Business focus

Measure response time, appointment/order completion, exceptions, handoff quality and adoption. These are evaluation priorities; no unverified savings or conversion results are claimed here.
